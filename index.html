Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask

What areas does Amazonia Floors serve?

We serve homeowners and businesses across Baltimore County, Baltimore City, and Harford County, Maryland — including Towson, Perry Hall, and surrounding communities.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes — free, no-obligation in-home estimates. Call 410-841-9780 or request one online and we'll respond within one business day.

Should I refinish or replace my hardwood floors?

In most cases solid hardwood can be refinished rather than replaced — saving money while restoring a like-new finish. Our dust-controlled process handles stairs, landings, and hard-to-reach areas.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Amazonia Floors is fully licensed and insured for complete peace of mind on every project.

What types of flooring do you install?

Solid and engineered hardwood, luxury vinyl plank (LVP/LVT), ceramic tile and natural stone, and carpet — for both residential and commercial spaces.
